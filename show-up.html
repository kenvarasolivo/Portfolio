Show Up.

Making it easier to discover plans and meet people offline.

I took Show Up from product concept through UX, branding, frontend, backend, deployment, and iteration.

Explore live product↗ Read the story
RoleIndependent creator and engineer
ScopeProduct · UX · Frontend · Backend · Deployment
Built withNext.js · TypeScript · Neon Postgres · MapLibre

01 / Context

From online discovery to showing up in person.

Show Up is a social app built around a simple idea: help people find plans worth joining and meet others offline. I shaped the concept and the experience, then built and deployed the working product myself.

02 / Product decisions

An MVP focused on the next real-world step.

01

Start with plans

Make discovering something to do the center of the experience, so the product has a clear purpose from the first screen.

02

Keep the experience approachable

Use clear language, considered visual design, and responsive flows to reduce friction between interest and action.

03

Own the whole journey

Connect product decisions to implementation, deployment, and iteration instead of treating the interface as a standalone mockup.

03 / What shipped

A live product, built end to end.

Show Up is the clearest example of how I work: define the product, design the experience, build the system, put it online, and keep improving it.
