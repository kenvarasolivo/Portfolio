Team of four · Applied AI commerce

Chattrolley.

A conversational storefront that helps shoppers get from questions to checkout.

Built with a four-person team, Chattrolley connects an AI sales assistant to store data so it can recommend products, check inventory, display relevant items, and direct customers to checkout.

Explore live product↗ Read the story
RoleFour-person product team
ScopeAI assistant · Store data · Product discovery
Built withNext.js · TypeScript · Gemini · Drizzle

01 / Context

Product discovery is a conversation.

Shoppers often need help narrowing choices before they are ready to buy. Chattrolley explores how a conversational interface can make that journey clearer while staying connected to a real store's catalog and availability.

02 / Product decisions

AI grounded in the shopping flow.

01

Use store data

Recommendations should relate to products the store actually carries, with availability checked as part of the conversation.

02

Show the product

Bring product details into the interface so shoppers can inspect options as the assistant explains them.

03

Keep checkout connected

The conversation is useful when it helps customers make a decision and gives them a clear route to purchase.

03 / What shipped

A working AI commerce experience.

Chattrolley brings recommendations, inventory checks, product display, and checkout direction into one storefront experience. It remains an early-stage team project.
